<h1>About LabSens</h1>
<p>LabSens was founded in 2021 from the merger of the Electronics and Instrumentation Systems Lab (LABSEI, since ~1990) and the Acoustics and Vibration Lab (LAV, since 2016). It is part of PUCV’s School of Electrical Engineering.</p>
<p>Directed by <strong>Sebastián Fingerhuth</strong> and <strong>Daniel Yunge</strong>, the lab focuses on applied sensing, wireless sensor networks (WSN), low-power embedded systems, and signal processing, with interdisciplinary and international collaboration (Germany, Spain, UK).</p>
<p>A flagship line is <strong>light pollution</strong>: night-sky brightness measurement, spectral characterization, and enforcement tools, in partnership with PUCV’s Photometry Lab and public agencies.</p>
